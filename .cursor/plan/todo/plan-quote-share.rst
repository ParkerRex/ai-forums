I like the way substack does quote sharing. 

When a user highlights a block of text on a post detail, theres a tool tip that appears that shows a share icon and the word share. 

when a user clicks share, a share quote modal appears. 
You can see this interaction here: .cursor/plan/todo/share-quote-step-1.png

That modal has a top text that says share quote

The modal has sharable images that look really nice and follow the theme that the app uses. We'd use our monochromatic minimal style. 

You can see the modal here: .cursor/plan/todo/share-quote-step-2.png

You can also generate different sizes for the share quote. 

You can see the different sizes here: .cursor/plan/todo/share-quote-step-3.png

When you click a different backgroudn color or aspect ratio, the modal will update to show the different sizes. 

You can see the different sizes here: .cursor/plan/todo/share-quote-step-4.png

when you click download, a new tab quickly opens up and downloads the image, and names the file, then closes the tab and refocuses back to where you were.

You can see the download here: .cursor/plan/todo/share-quote-step-5.png
