== Context
This is a template for Architecture Decision Records (ADRs). ADRs are short text documents that record a significant architectural decision made about the system. An ADR describes a decision, its context, and its consequences.

== Decision
To adopt the Architecture Decision Record (ADR) process for documenting significant architectural decisions within this project. This will help in maintaining a historical log of decisions, providing context for future team members, and facilitating discussions around architectural changes.

== Consequences
*   **Positive:**
    *   Improved clarity and transparency around architectural choices.
    *   Easier onboarding for new team members who can review past decisions.
    *   Reduced risk of repeating past mistakes or re-litigating decisions.
    *   Provides a structured way to discuss and agree upon significant changes.
*   **Negative:**
    *   Requires discipline from the team to write and maintain ADRs.
    *   Initial overhead in learning the ADR process and AsciiDoc format.
    *   Potential for "analysis paralysis" if every minor decision is documented. (Mitigation: Focus only on *significant* architectural decisions.)
