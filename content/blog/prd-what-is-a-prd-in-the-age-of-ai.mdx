# PRD: What is a PRD in the Age of AI

The Product Requirements Document is dead. Long live the Product Requirements Document.

## The Traditional PRD

For decades, PRDs have been the cornerstone of product development:

- **Problem Statement**: What are we solving?
- **User Stories**: Who needs this and why?
- **Requirements**: What must it do?
- **Success Metrics**: How do we measure impact?
- **Timeline**: When will it ship?

A good PRD could take days to write. Multiple stakeholder reviews. Endless revisions.

## Enter AI

Now, an AI can generate a comprehensive PRD in 30 seconds. Fed with a few bullet points, it produces:

```markdown
# PRD: AI-Generated Social Feed

## Executive Summary
A personalized content feed leveraging ML algorithms...

## User Personas
1. Power User (Sarah, 28, Tech PM)
   - Needs: Efficiency, customization
   - Pain points: Information overload
   
2. Casual Browser (Mike, 45, Sales)
   - Needs: Simple discovery
   - Pain points: Missing important updates

## Functional Requirements
- REQ-001: Feed must load in <2s
- REQ-002: Support infinite scroll
- REQ-003: Real-time updates
[... 47 more requirements ...]
```

Looks perfect. Hits all the checkboxes. Completely useless.

## The Problem with AI PRDs

AI excels at form, struggles with soul. It can generate:

- ✅ Proper structure
- ✅ Industry jargon
- ✅ Plausible requirements
- ❌ Deep user insight
- ❌ Strategic vision
- ❌ The "why" behind decisions

## The New Role of PRDs

In the AI age, PRDs must evolve from documentation to thinking tools:

### 1. Context Machines

Not "what" but "why now":

```markdown
## Why This, Why Now

Competitor X just raised $50M for this exact space.
Our user interviews show 73% frustration with current solution.
Engineering has 2 sprints of availability before Q2 planning.
CEO mentioned this problem in 3 all-hands.
```

### 2. Decision Logs

Document the debates:

```markdown
## Key Decisions

**Real-time vs. Batch Processing**
- Considered: Real-time everything
- Decided: Batch for analytics, real-time for notifications
- Reason: 10x cost difference, 100ms UX difference
- Dissent: Mobile team wanted full real-time
```

### 3. Constraint Maps

What we're NOT doing:

```markdown
## Out of Scope (and Why)

- Multi-language support (wait for v2)
- Video uploads (infrastructure not ready)
- External integrations (security review = 3 months)
- Desktop app (mobile-first strategy)
```

## The Human Touch

What AI can't generate:

**Taste**: Why this shade of blue? Why 48px not 50px? Why "Start" not "Begin"?

**Conviction**: The willingness to say "users are wrong about wanting this feature."

**Context**: Knowing the CEO hates modals because of a bad experience in 2019.

**Politics**: Feature X exists because Sales promised it to close MegaCorp.

## A New Framework

Modern PRDs should be:

1. **Living Documents**: Updated daily, not quarterly
2. **Decision-First**: Focus on choices made, not features listed
3. **Context-Rich**: More "why" than "what"
4. **AI-Assisted**: Use AI for structure, humans for substance

## The Future

AI will write the PRD. Humans will write the PRD brief.

The brief: 200 words of pure context, constraint, and conviction. The essence that no AI can fake.

```markdown
## PRD Brief: Social Feed Redesign

We're losing 1,000 DAUs/week to TikTok-style feeds.
Board wants "innovation" for next meeting (Feb 15).
Must work with existing MongoDB (no migration budget).
Sales promised "AI-powered" to Enterprise clients.
No infinite scroll - CEO thinks it's "slot machine dark patterns."
Mobile-first but Desktop VP will riot if broken.
Success = Jane from Customer Success stops complaining.
```

Feed this to AI. Get your PRD. Add your soul back in.

## Conclusion

The PRD isn't dead. It's evolving from documentation to communication tool. From feature list to decision log. From what we're building to why we're building it.

In the age of AI, the most human documents become the most valuable.
